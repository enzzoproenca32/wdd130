Beira Sofala

Beira, Sofala, is my favorite city because of its beautiful beaches, warm weather, and peaceful atmosphere. It is a special place with friendly people, beautiful landscapes, and a unique culture. I especially love the feeling of being close to the ocean and enjoying the simple moments in the city.

Beira is also very special to me because it is where I served my mission. During my time there, I met many wonderful people and had many experiences that I will never forget. I spent my days helping others, learning new things, and sharing meaningful moments with the people around me. I was very happy during that time, and the memories I made in Beira are some of the most important memories of my life.
